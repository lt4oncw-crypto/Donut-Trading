Common Questions

How do I start trading?

Join our official Discord server and head to the #trading-center channel. From there, you can list your items or browse active offers from other elite players.

Is trading 100% safe?

We use a custom-built, secure trading plugin on the server that prevents scams by requiring both parties to confirm the trade before items are exchanged.

What are Gems used for?

Gems are the primary currency of Donut SMP. You can use them to buy rare donuts, upgrade your rank, or trade for other valuable in-game resources.
